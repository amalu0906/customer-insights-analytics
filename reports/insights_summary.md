# 📊 Insights Summary

This section highlights the key findings derived from the customer insights dashboard, along with their business implications.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* A small group of high-value customer segments contributes a significant share of total revenue, indicating strong revenue concentration.
* A notable proportion of customers fall into “At Risk” and “Lost” categories, highlighting clear opportunities for targeted retention strategies.
* Revenue trends show variability across months, suggesting potential seasonality and changes in customer purchasing behaviour.
* High-value segments account for a disproportionate share of revenue compared to the rest of the customer base.

---

## 💡 Business Implications

* Prioritise retention strategies for high-value customers to protect overall revenue.
* Target “At Risk” customers with timely engagement (e.g. offers, personalised communication).
* Use customer segmentation to personalise marketing and improve conversion rates.
* Monitor monthly trends to anticipate fluctuations and optimise business planning.

---

## 🧠 Summary

This analysis demonstrates how combining transaction data, segmentation, and churn logic can provide actionable insights to support customer-focused business decisions.
